Warranty statement

Built to stand behind our work.

This statement explains our standard limited workmanship warranty. Your signed estimate or contract identifies whether the warranty applies to your project and controls if its terms differ from this page.

Warranty coverage applies only to qualifying work specifically performed by Simple Screen Service and paid in full.

1. Limited workmanship coverage

For qualifying projects, Simple Screen Service warrants that its installation workmanship will be free from defects for three years from substantial completion. Covered issues may include workmanship-related screen detachment, improper screen tension, improperly installed spline, or installation-related hardware problems in work we performed.

2. Who and what qualifies

Coverage is available to the original customer at the original service address when the signed estimate or contract states that the three-year workmanship warranty applies. The project account must be paid in full. Coverage is not transferable unless Simple Screen Service approves the transfer in writing.

3. Materials and manufacturer warranties

This is a workmanship warranty. Screen mesh, aluminum, fasteners, doors, hardware and other products may carry separate manufacturer warranties. Manufacturer coverage, exclusions and remedies are controlled by the manufacturer. We will reasonably assist the original customer with an eligible manufacturer claim when documentation is available.

4. What is not covered

The limited workmanship warranty does not cover damage or failure caused by:

  • Hurricanes, tropical storms, tornadoes, hail, lightning, flooding, high winds or other acts of nature.
  • Falling trees or debris, animals, pets, pests, vandalism, accidents, misuse or impact.
  • Pressure washing, harsh chemicals, painting, alterations, repairs or work performed by others.
  • Normal wear, aging, fading, corrosion, mildew, environmental exposure or routine maintenance.
  • Structural movement, settlement, pre-existing conditions or components outside our contracted scope.
  • Customer-supplied materials or a customer’s failure to follow care and maintenance instructions.

5. Reporting and inspection

The customer must report a suspected covered condition promptly after discovery, provide reasonable documentation and allow access for inspection. Submission of a claim does not establish coverage. We will determine whether the condition is covered after reviewing project records and, when necessary, inspecting the work.

6. Warranty remedy

For an approved claim, Simple Screen Service may repair or re-perform the affected covered workmanship at its option. This limited remedy does not include cash reimbursement, work by another contractor without our prior written authorization, or incidental or consequential damages to the extent permitted by law.

7. Other rights

This warranty gives the customer specific contractual rights. Other rights may apply under Florida law. Nothing in this statement excludes a right or remedy that cannot legally be excluded.
